## About FTX

FTX is a cryptocurrency based  futures exchange focused on developing digital asset trading products, financial technology, and trading infrastructure.

We company aim to provide innovative solutions for cryptocurrency markets through technology, product development, and financial infrastructure.

Our work covers several areas of the digital asset ecosystem, including trading systems, market data, APIs, and developer tools.

## Our Vision

To contribute to the evolution of digital technology and innovation through financial markets.

We believe in building accessible, efficient, and scalable technology for the global digital asset ecosystem.

## Technology & Infrastructure

Our technology initiatives included:

- **Trading Infrastructure** — Systems supporting digital asset trading.
- **API Development** — Interfaces for programmatic trading and integrations.
- **Market Data** — Tools and infrastructure for accessing market information.
- **Financial Technology** — Software solutions for digital asset markets.
- **Developer Tools** — Resources and integrations for software developers.
